# S002 always-on-budget

Default severity: **warning**

## What it flags

The estimated tokens loaded every session, per tool, exceed the budget (default 2,000; `budget.always_on_tokens` in `.kogentic-rules-lint.yaml`). The message lists each file's share.

## Why it matters

Large always-on context dilutes every instruction in it and costs tokens on every request.

## Bad

A 12 KB CLAUDE.md plus two imported files (about 4,000 tokens for Claude Code).

## Good

A short CLAUDE.md with project-wide rules, and area rules in scoped `.claude/rules/*.md` files.

## How to fix

Tokens are estimated as bytes / 4 and counted per tool (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf, Cline, Roo, Gemini, AGENTS.md). Move area-specific sections into scoped rules (S001), delete filler (V002), or raise the budget.

## Turn it off

In `.kogentic-rules-lint.yaml`:

```yaml
rules:
  S002: off
```

Or inline, for one line: `<!-- rules-lint-disable-next-line S002 -->`.
